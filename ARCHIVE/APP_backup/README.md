# 📱 Chit Fund Payment Tracker & WhatsApp Notifier

A complete interactive Web App to track monthly chit payments with **Tick Boxes (PAID / PENDING)**, **Real-Time Live Counters**, and **1-Click WhatsApp Group Notices** for all 4 Chits.

---

## 🚀 How to Use

### 1. Open on Computer (PC / Laptop):
Simply double-click:
👉 **`Open_Chit_Tracker.bat`** (in `d:\CHIT`)  
*OR* open **`APP\index.html`** in any browser (Edge, Chrome, Brave).

---

### 2. Open on Mobile Phone (Same Wi-Fi):
1. Double-click **`APP\Run_Server_For_Mobile.bat`**.
2. It will display your computer's IP address (e.g., `http://192.168.1.15:8080`).
3. Open that link in your phone's browser (Safari or Chrome).
4. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"** on your phone to use it like a real mobile app icon!

---

### 3. Free Cloud Hosting (Access Anywhere from Phone):
You can host this for **100% free forever** on **Vercel** or **Netlify**:
1. Go to [netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) or [vercel.com](https://vercel.com/).
2. Drag and drop the **`MAIN`** folder (`d:\CHIT\MAIN`) directly into the browser.
3. You will get a private live URL (e.g., `https://my-chit-tracker.netlify.app`) that you can bookmark on your phone!

---

## ✨ Features

- **All 4 Chits Included:**
  1. Ganapathi Chit (₹ 2,40,000)
  2. Shiva Chit (₹ 1,20,000)
  3. Venkateshwara Chit (₹ 3,00,000)
  4. Ranganadh Chit (₹ 3,60,000)
- **Satisfying Tick Boxes:** 1-click toggles between **PAID** (green) and **PENDING** (red).
- **Payment Method Badges:** Choose PhonePe/UPI, Cash, or Bank Transfer.
- **Live Summary Cards:** Real-time Collected Amount, Pending Balance, and Progress Bar.
- **1-Click WhatsApp Copy:**
  - 📢 **Copy Group Notice:** Creates full formatted announcement with list of amounts and UPI ID.
  - ⚠️ **Copy Pending List:** Creates a list of only pending members to nudge in group.
- **Offline Auto-Save:** All data automatically saves to your browser's local storage and stays there even when closed.
